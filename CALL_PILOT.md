# Opt-in project support call pilot

## Objective

Test whether a person who voluntarily follows this project wants a short, manual conversation about the starter kit, its roadmap, and ways to support continued development.

## Boundaries

- Opt-in interest only; no cold calls, purchased lists, scraped numbers, autodialing, prerecorded messages, or AI-generated voice calls.
- Never collect phone numbers, addresses, credentials, payment information, or recordings through a public GitHub issue.
- The public form does not itself authorize a call. A secure scheduling and consent step must occur first.
- No call recording.
- Identify the caller and project immediately, confirm the person requested the conversation, and offer an easy way to end the call.
- Do not claim charitable or tax-exempt status. Any future support is voluntary project support and is not represented as tax-deductible.
- Never pressure, shame, create false urgency, promise returns, or fabricate users, expenses, milestones, or impact.
- Never collect card, bank, login, or recovery information by phone.
- No payment link or request is sent until the project owner separately approves the exact platform, terms, amount framing, and recipient workflow.

## Proposed 10-minute call structure

1. Identify the caller and project; confirm the person opted in and still wants the call.
2. Ask what interested them about offline AI and listen.
3. Explain the verified starter-kit work and current limitations in plain language.
4. Ask whether they want to support continued open-source development; make clear that support is optional and not tax-deductible.
5. If they decline, thank them and end the request immediately.
6. If they express interest, do not take payment information. Record only the consented outcome and return to the project owner for the next approval step.

## Draft script

> Hi, this is Andrew's Charlie Offline project team. You expressed interest through our GitHub call-pilot form. Is this still a good time for a brief conversation? You can stop the call at any point.
>
> We published a free Windows starter kit for experimenting with local speech recognition, local language models, and local speech. We are validating what people find useful and what should be improved. What interested you about the project?
>
> Thank you. Continued development takes testing and documentation time. If you find the work useful, would you be interested in voluntarily supporting further open-source development? This is not a charity or tax-deductible contribution, and there is no obligation.
>
> If no: Absolutely—thank you for the feedback and your time.
>
> If yes: Thank you. We do not take payment details by phone. I will record that you are interested, and the project owner will review the appropriate secure next step before anything is sent.

## Success criteria

- First validation success: one genuine public opt-in from a person who understands the boundaries.
- Call activation success: one separately consented, manually supervised call using a verified calling account.
- Revenue success: only a real, recorded payment after platform, tax, and approval checks—not expressed interest or a promise.
